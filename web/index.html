How it works

  1. Fees in. 100% of creator fees are sent to the gacha contract as $EARN and go straight into the prize pool.
  2. Hold & join. Hold at least the minimum $EARN and submit your wallet address. Once per batch.
  3. Every 30 minutes. When the batch closes, the gacha is pulled. The contract picks 10 of that batch's wallets with NEAR's on-chain randomness.
  4. Paid in $EARN. Each pick still holding the minimum gets an equal share of 100% of the pool, sent straight to their wallet.
