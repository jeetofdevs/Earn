How it works

  1. Fees in. 100% of $EARN tax fees go to the fee wallet. The creator keeps nothing.
  2. Snapshot. At payout time, every holder's $EARN balance is read on-chain.
  3. Pro-rata. Your share = your balance ÷ all holders' balance. Hold more, earn more.
  4. Paid. Your share of the pool is sent to your wallet in $EARN. Nothing to claim.
