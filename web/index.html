How it works

  1. Fees in. The token's creator wallet is this contract. Every trade fee goes straight into the prize pool.
  2. Holders enter. Hold at least the minimum tokens and enter once (0.01 NEAR deposit, refunded when you leave).
  3. Pull. When the timer hits zero anyone can pull the gacha. 10 entrants are picked at random.
  4. Paid. Each pick still holding gets an equal share of 100% of the pool. Anyone who sold is dropped and their share goes to the others.
